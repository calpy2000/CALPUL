What is PUSULZ?

PUSULZ is a hub of small daily games — a mix of word games, number games, visual puzzles, and a few that combine all of the above. Every game gives you exactly one attempt per day. Once you've played it (won, lost, or run out of time), that's it until tomorrow, when a brand new challenge is waiting.

Nothing to repeat, nothing to keep playing over and over — just a couple of minutes a day, then come back tomorrow.

Your role as a tester

  • Play as often as you can — even just one or two games a day. Since each game only allows one go per day, the more days you play, the more we learn.
  • Try to play all 8 games at least once over the coming days, not just your favourites — we need eyes on every game.
  • Tell us anything — no feedback is too small. That includes things like:
    • Bugs, confusing bits, or things that felt too easy or too hard
    • Ideas to make an existing game better — a rule that feels unfair, a visual that's hard to read, a difficulty curve that spikes too fast, anything you'd tweak if it were up to you
    • Ideas for new gameplay — a new obstacle, a new twist on an existing game, or even a whole new game concept
  • Please at least try and give us some feedback at the end of your game play for the day — this will automatically tell us how many games you played and how you did in each game — just select OVERALL DAILY FEEDBACK in the Type dropdown (it should be the default anyway), add just a bit of feedback and hit send. So 10 seconds max — it will be SOOOO helpful 🤩

How to give feedback

Every page has a small 🧪 icon in the bottom-right corner — Tester tools. Tap it, then "Send feedback."

This opens your mail app with a form already partly filled in (which game, your device, today's scores) — just add your comment and hit send.

Testing tools

Tap that same 🧪 icon any time to open:

  • Send feedback — jump straight to the feedback form (see above).
  • Reset today's progress — this one's special to you as a tester. Normal players only get one go per game per day, full stop. But you're not a normal player — reset lets you replay any game as many times as you like, the same day. Found a bug halfway through? Want to try a different approach? Just tap reset and go again. Don't be shy about using it — the whole point is to put each game through its paces, not to play it once and move on.
  • See tester instructions — brings back everything on this page, any time, without needing to dig up this message again. Forgot how to give feedback, or what reset actually does? Check here.

The panel also shows the current app version number — worth mentioning if you're reporting a bug, so we know exactly which version you were on.

WhatsApp group

We'll also add you to our PUSULZ beta testers WhatsApp group — this will allow us to get important information and questions out to the whole group, as well as letting you vote on key design decisions based on the group's feedback, and complete some quick surveys now and again.

Getting set up

The steps are different depending on your phone — find iPhone or Android below and follow just that one section, not both.

Getting set up (iPhone)

Read all three steps below before you tap the link in step 1 — once you tap it, you'll leave this page and won't come back to it.

Heads up: the link will show a message about installing PUSULZ rather than the games themselves — that's expected, not a bug. You'll only be asked for your entry code once, after step 2, when you open your new home screen tile for the first time.

Step 1 — tap your link

Only tap this on the device you intend to test on:

https://calpy2000.github.io/PUSULZ/

You'll land on a page telling you to add PUSULZ to your Home Screen. Stay on it for step 2.

Step 2 — add PUSULZ to your Home Screen

Tap the Share icon in Safari (square with an arrow, along the bottom of the screen), then scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen," then "Add" in the top-right. You'll get a proper PUSULZ app icon on your home screen.

Step 3 — open your new tile and enter your code

Tap the PUSULZ tile you just created. Enter the entry code you were sent when asked. From then on, always launch PUSULZ from this tile, and your device will remember your code — no more typing it in. You may occasionally need to re-enter it again after that, e.g. following a major update to the app, but not for normal day-to-day play.

Getting set up (Android)

Read both steps below before you tap the link in step 1 — once you tap it, you'll leave this page and won't come back to it. These steps are for Chrome (the default on most Android phones) — another browser will have a similar option, just in a different menu.

Step 1 — tap your link

Only tap this on the device you intend to test on:

https://calpy2000.github.io/PUSULZ/

You'll land on a page telling you to add PUSULZ to your home screen — that's expected, not a bug. Stay on it for step 2.

Step 2 — add PUSULZ to your home screen

Tap the ⋮ menu (three dots, top-right of Chrome), then tap "Add to Home screen" (some phones show "Install app" instead), then confirm with "Add" or "Install." You'll get a proper PUSULZ app icon on your home screen.

If Chrome shows its own pop-up suggesting you install PUSULZ before you even get to the menu, you can tap that instead — same result.

Step 3 — open your new tile and enter your code

Tap the PUSULZ tile you just created. Enter the entry code you were sent when asked — you won't be asked again after that.
